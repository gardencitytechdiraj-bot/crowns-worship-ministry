01Who we are

A worship movement with both feet on the ground.

Crown's Worship Ministry is headquartered in Ranipouwa, Pokhara — a home base, a training ground, and the place where every journey begins.

We travel across Nepal to train worship teams, mentor new leaders, and serve families in need. We believe worship is more than a moment on a stage. It is a way of living that makes room for courage, care, and community.
